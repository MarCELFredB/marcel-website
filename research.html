Research

Our Research Projects

MarCEL conducts cutting-edge research on marine conservation ecology across multiple global locations. Our work combines field studies, advanced tracking technologies, and spatial analysis to inform conservation strategies and policy.

Active Research Themes

Our research spans four main themes:

1. Sea Turtle Ecology & Conservation
Long-distance movement tracking, habitat use, and conservation strategies for endangered sea turtle populations across global ocean basins.

2. Marine Habitat Discovery & Characterization
Discovery and ecological assessment of previously unknown habitats including deep-water seagrass meadows and mesophotic coral reefs.

3. Fish Spatial Ecology
Understanding movement patterns, habitat preferences, and population dynamics of fish species in both protected and unprotected areas.

4. Conservation Planning & Management
Translating research findings into actionable conservation strategies, contributing to Marine Protected Area design and management plans.

Research Locations

Our field work spans from local Welsh waters to tropical oceans: - Bristol Channel & Welsh Coast - Local marine biodiversity - Mediterranean Sea - Sea turtle nesting and foraging grounds - Indian Ocean - Large-scale turtle migrations - Caribbean - Coral reef and seagrass ecosystems - Red Sea - Unique marine fauna and habitats

Collaborative Research

We work with partners worldwide including: - Natural Resources Wales - Marine Conservation Society - University of Exeter - International marine research institutions - Local conservation organizations
