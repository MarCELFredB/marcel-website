Our Team

Dr Nicole Esteban

Principal Investigator / Associate Professor

Nicole leads the Marine Conservation Ecology Lab with 20+ years of experience in tropical coastal ecosystems and integrated coastal zone management. Her research focuses on spatial and habitat use by marine fauna, particularly sea turtles and fisheries. She has extensive experience managing Marine Parks and National Parks in the Caribbean, and her current work spans the Indian Ocean, Caribbean, Red Sea, Mediterranean, and locally in Swansea Bay.
