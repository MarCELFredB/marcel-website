About MarCEL

Welcome to the Marine Conservation Ecology Lab (MarCEL)

We research spatial and habitat use by marine fauna, with a primary focus on sea turtles and fish. Our work is based locally in Wales and the Bristol Channel as well as further afield in the Indian Ocean, Caribbean, Red Sea and Mediterranean, and we collaborate with researchers around the world.

Our Research Focus

Sea Turtle Conservation

We conduct long-term tracking studies of sea turtle movements across ocean basins, contributing to international conservation efforts and Marine Protected Area design.

Marine Habitat Mapping

Our team has contributed to major discoveries including deep-water seagrass meadows and mesophotic reefs, expanding our understanding of marine biodiversity.

Fish Spatial Ecology

We investigate how fish utilize marine environments, particularly within Marine Protected Areas, to inform effective conservation planning.

Conservation Planning

Our research directly contributes to MPA designations, habitat management strategies, and conservation policy at local, national, and international scales.

Location

We are based in the School of Biosciences, Geography and Physics at Swansea University, Wales, UK.
